About ReNewMarket

ReNewMarket is an online marketplace that connects buyers and sellers of second-hand goods: electronics, furniture, clothing and collectibles. Sellers create listings with descriptions, photos and prices; buyers search, filter, negotiate, pay securely and arrange pick-up or delivery. This admin panel manages the platform's data.
