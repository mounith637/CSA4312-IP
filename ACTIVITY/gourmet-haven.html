Our Story

About Gourmet Haven

A passion since 1998

Gourmet Haven opened in 1998 as a 20-seat bistro with one simple belief: great food brings people together. Over 25 years we have grown into one of the city's most loved fine dining destinations, without losing the intimacy of that first dining room.

Our concept: modern European technique meets Indian soul. Every menu is built around the seasons and the farmers, fishermen and artisans we know by name.

Culinary philosophy: respect the ingredient, cook with patience, and serve with warmth. We make our stocks, breads, pastas and ice creams in-house every single day.
