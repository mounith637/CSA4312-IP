Online Appointment Booking System for MedCare Hospital

Name: K. MounithReg. No: 192425090Course: Mobile Computing (ITA03)Institution: SIMATS Engineering, Saveetha School of EngineeringFaculty Guide: Dr. Ajitha Rajagopal
Aim and Abstract

Aim: To design a web-based system that lets patients search hospitals, book and manage doctor appointments, and lets hospitals and admins manage doctors, appointments and ratings.

Abstract: The system reduces waiting time and paperwork by moving appointment booking online. It has three roles: User/Patient, Hospital and Admin. Patients register, search by location or specialty, view the hospital list and Google Map, book a slot, submit a check-in form, track status and review doctors. Hospitals register, create and update their details and location, and manage doctors and appointments. Admins view statistics, manage appointments and doctors, and see rating details. Built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript; data is stored in the browser (localStorage).

Modules
  • User: registration, login, location-wise search, hospital list, book appointment, view status, Google Map
  • Hospital: registration, login, create details, update details, update location
  • Admin: manage appointments, manage doctors, appointment statistics, rating details
  • Patient: choose date and time, search by specialty and location, reviews, past check-in and appointment history
